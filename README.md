# AI Vendor Trust Pack

This project is a sample security questionnaire response pack for a fictional AI-for-finance vendor called FinSight AI. The buyer is a bank conducting vendor diligence. It is designed to illustrate how customer trust and security assurance teams at enterprise AI companies prepare for, and respond to, the security and governance questions that financial-services buyers ask before signing a contract. The project covers 25 bank diligence questions organized across six areas, sample vendor responses designed to reflect what strong answers look like, a control-theme mapping connecting those questions to relevant frameworks, an evidence checklist for AI vendor diligence preparation, and supporting notes for learning and interview preparation.

## Why banks ask detailed security and AI governance questions

Banks are regulated entities. Their regulators -- including the OCC, FDIC, Federal Reserve, PRA, and ECB SSM -- require them to assess and manage the risk posed by third-party vendors before onboarding them. This requirement applies to all software vendors. For AI vendors, the diligence scope extends beyond standard security questions into areas that most banks are still developing internal playbooks for: whether customer data is used to train AI models, how tenant isolation is enforced at the AI layer, how model outputs are logged for audit and regulatory purposes, whether the product is classified appropriately under frameworks like the EU AI Act, and what controls exist to prevent AI-specific attacks like prompt injection. A bank that cannot answer these questions to its regulator's satisfaction has a vendor risk problem. A vendor that cannot answer them clearly has a deal problem.

Nothing in this project constitutes legal, security, or compliance advice. Any sample responses would require review and validation by qualified security, legal, and compliance professionals before use in a real vendor diligence process.
